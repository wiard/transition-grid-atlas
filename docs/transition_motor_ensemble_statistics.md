# Transition Motor Ensemble Statistics

## Purpose
Audit whether ensemble improvements are robust across samples or driven by outliers.

## Metrics
- detector win-rate
- noise-action win-rate
- leakage win-rate
- objective win-rate
- bootstrap confidence intervals
- bound-pressure summary
- tradeoff correlations

## Interpretation
A positive mean detector gain is stronger when supported by win-rate and confidence intervals.

## Caution
Synthetic ensemble only. Not experimental validation. Not full QEC.
