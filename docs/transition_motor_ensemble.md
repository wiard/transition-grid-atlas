# Transition Motor Ensemble Study

## Purpose
Test transition-motor robustness over synthetic wafer disorder and phase-noise ensembles.

## Relation to transition motor
Uses explicit knob registry, control bases, `H(theta)`, dynamic transport subspace and noise-action metrics.

## Detector-output metrics
Adds detector success probability and final target probability.

## Fixed-grid assumption
No physical layout changes and no new edges.

## Synthetic wafer disorder
Onsite propagation-constant shifts.

## Phase-noise profiles
Diagonal noise operators.

## Success criterion
Objective gain, detector-success gain and noise-action reduction.

## Bound-pressure context
Report saturated and near-bound knobs per sample.

## Scientific caution
Synthetic ensemble validation, not experimental validation.
Hardware-native error suppression, not full QEC.
